# LxTerm privacy policy

LxTerm does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- Debian runs entirely on your device, inside the app's own storage. Your files, installed packages and command history stay there; deleting the app deletes them.
- **Network.** The app itself contacts no server. Programs you run in the terminal connect where you tell them to: for example `apt` downloads packages from Debian's mirrors (deb.debian.org), `ssh` and `curl` reach the hosts you type. Those connections are yours, as on any computer.
- DNS servers are read from your iPhone's settings so programs in the terminal can look up names.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/lxterm/issues
