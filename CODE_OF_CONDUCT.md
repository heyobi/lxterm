# Code of conduct

Pocket Distro is built in the open and welcomes everyone who wants to use it, report a problem or contribute, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussions about the work: code, measurements, documentation, how it behaves on a device.
- Give and take criticism of ideas, not of people. Say what you measured and how.
- Help newcomers find their way; a question asked once is fine to answer again.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Unwanted sexual attention, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository and its issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@pocketdistro.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who do not follow this code, in proportion to what happened.

This code draws on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
