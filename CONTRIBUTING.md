# Contributing to LxTerm

Thanks for helping! LxTerm is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. Its `core/` is shared with LiquidAPK
(github.com/heyobi/apk-on-iphone), which takes contributions on the same terms.

## Why there is a contributor agreement

The official LxTerm builds are distributed across various platforms and digital
storefronts (including the Apple App Store and other app stores, marketplaces, or direct
distribution channels). Because proprietary app store and commercial distribution terms
are not fully compatible with pure GPL requirements, the maintainers must have the legal
right to distribute, monetize, and package contributed code in official builds under store
and platform terms as well.

By opening a pull request you agree that:

1. Your contribution is your own work (or you have the legal right to submit it), and
   you license it to the project under **GPL-3.0-or-later**.
2. You additionally grant the LxTerm maintainers a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide,
   transferable, sub-licensable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, compile,
   publish, monetize, and distribute your contribution (as source code, binaries, or services)
   as part of official LxTerm releases across any platforms, distribution channels,
   and app stores (including the Apple App Store and any other marketplaces) under any
   terms chosen by the maintainers.
3. You sign off each commit (`git commit -s`) to certify the
   [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/).

Your contribution always stays available to everyone in this repository under the GPL.
